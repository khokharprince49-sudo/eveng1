3. Offline glasses dashboard

Save up to four Quick Notes on this phone, then sync them to the G1's built-in dashboard. After syncing, the notes are stored on the glasses and can be browsed from the glasses' own dashboard while your phone is disconnected. New changes sync the next time both lenses connect.
